Early First Trimester Prognosis Calculator*

Based on Doubilet et al. J Ultrasound Med 2021; 40: 541-550

Probability of Viability at the End of the First Trimester
--

* Applies to early pregnancies with ultrasound showing saclike intrauterine structure without yolk sac or embryo.

Calculator should not be used when serial hCG values are unchanged or decreasing.

Serial hCG Trend is “Appropriate” if initial hCG is <5000 and at least doubles in 2 days; “Suboptimal” if initial hCG is <5000 and fails to double in 2 days; “Not measured” if initial hCG >5000 or serial hCG's were not done.
